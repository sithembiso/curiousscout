✨ 0.7 — BIOMES OF DARKNESS (the game you start in)
  • 🌍 AN ENDLESS WORLD — the Overworld never ends, and 1,000 blocks out it breaks into the Far Lands 🌌
  • 🏜️ BIOMES — plains, forests, deserts, snowy taiga and towering mountains
  • 🏘️ VILLAGES with friendly villagers and chests full of food, supplies and weapons 🍞⚔️
  • 🌙 DAY & NIGHT every 5 minutes — and at night the ZOMBIES come out 🧟
  • 💾 WORLDS & SEEDS — save several worlds, pick any seed, play Creative 🎨 or Hardcore 💀
  • 🧱 8×8 block textures, /tp and other commands, food, and 5 new achievements
  • 🥛 THE LAND OF MILK & HONEY is now a place to explore — 7 wonders to discover (Honey Falls, the Milk Lake, the Great Honey Tree, the Giant Hive 🐝, the Cloud Garden, the Flower Meadow and the Crystal Grotto) and 12 golden honeypots to find. No puzzles: find every wonder and the Guardian crowns you!
  • 🌑 Don't go too far. Something is out there.
🧪 TRY 0.8 — WATER & WONDERS (⚙️ Settings → Version 0.8)
  • 🌊 WATER! Lakes, rivers and seas, sandy beaches and frozen ponds. Swim (hold jump to paddle up) — but watch your 🫧 air
  • 🎣 FISHING — craft a rod, look at water, press G. Cook your catch in a furnace!
  • 🌾 FARMING — find seeds in the grass, plant them near water (plants need a drink!), harvest wheat, bake bread. Bone meal makes them grow at once 🦴
  • 🕳️ CAVES to explore under the ground — and they're DARK. 🔥 Torches light them up (and keep monsters away)
  • 💀🕷️💥 NEW MONSTERS — skeletons that shoot arrows, spiders that climb walls, and creepers that go BOOM
  • ♨️ FURNACE — sand into glass, wood into charcoal, raw fish into dinner
  • 🚪 BUILD MORE — doors (right-click to open), ladders, stairs you can walk up, slabs and planks
  • 🎒 INVENTORY (press I) — see everything you have and choose what's in your hotbar
  • 🔊 SOUND! Footsteps, splashes, moos, groans and booms (M to mute)
  • 💥 Falling hurts now — unless you land in water. And if you lose all your hearts, you just wake up in your bed with all your things 🛏️
  • 🏆 8 new achievements
🧪 OR 0.7.1 — TOPPED UP (⚙️ Settings → Version 0.7.1)
  • 🐄 FARM ANIMALS — cows, sheep and chickens in the grasslands. Click a cow for milk 🥛, a chicken for an egg 🥚, a sheep for wool 🐑 (it grows back!)
  • 🛏️ BEDS — craft one from 3 wool + 3 wood, click it at night and sleep till morning
  • 🌧️ WEATHER — rain showers, and snow in the taiga and up on the peaks 🌨️ (try /weather rain)
  • 🎒 A SMARTER HOTBAR — new blocks you pick up (sand, planks, glass, wool…) jump into an empty slot so you can build with them
  • 🏆 3 new achievements
